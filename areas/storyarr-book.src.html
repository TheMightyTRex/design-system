What changed and why

Two main actions (Request ebook, Add to list) and one More menu for the rest, instead of up to eight buttons with Delete beside the primary.

The tabs move to header row 2 with icons: Overview, Editions and files, Sources, History, plus a visible Details and matching tab for administrators (today a hidden eighth tab behind a gear). Authors and Reviews fold into Overview.

Slide-outs replace modals for a library copy, requesting an edition, release details (its tabs become sections) and matching, so you can compare rows. Delete, Download anyway and Add to list stay dialogs.

One filter bar on Sources (the section toolbar) instead of two stacked bars and a coverage block. No capitals in the eyebrow; facts list Author first, then Series and Narrated by.
