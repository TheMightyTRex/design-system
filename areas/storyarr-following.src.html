What changed and why

Authors, Series and Releases moved to header row 2 with icons, like every other page. The hero and the eyebrow "YOUR READING WORLD" are gone; the page has one h1.

One toolbar replaces the hand-built bar, the Show select and the separate sort: status chips with counts, sort, and Follow an author at the end.

Follow an author opens a slide-out with a search, instead of jumping to a books search.

No drawer inside the detail: a book opens the shared Summary slide-out. Follow settings open in a slide-out instead of swapping the whole page. One way to load more.
