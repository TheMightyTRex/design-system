# Storyarr boards (archived)

Storyarr was retired on 11 November 2026 and these boards were archived on 26 November 2026. They are a record of its interface, not a pattern to copy. Nobody updates them. The full history stays in git.

- `index-storyarr.html` lists the boards.
- `storyarr-<name>.html` are the boards; `.src.html` are their sources; `storyarr-gen/` is the script that wrote them (it no longer runs from here).
- The old addresses, such as `areas/storyarr-series.html` and `areas/index-storyarr.html`, are small pages that send a visitor here.
