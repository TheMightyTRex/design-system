What changed and why
  • Renamed Manage apps, so Apps means only the launcher. The 17-field modal is replaced by an add and edit slide-out in two clear steps: Connection, then Adding options.

  • Remove asks in a dialog, not in a toast that vanishes after 6 seconds.
