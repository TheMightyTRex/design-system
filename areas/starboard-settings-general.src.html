What changed and why
  • Follows the Settings group template: sections with an h2, a one-line description and their own Save, instead of one panel holding everything.
  • Categories were a comma-separated text box where renaming one silently moved apps. They are now an editable list with rename, reorder and remove; removing asks where the apps go.
  • The card options are switches that apply at once; the default design uses option cards.
