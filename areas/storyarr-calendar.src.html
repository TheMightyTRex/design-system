What changed and why

Day details use the shared slide-out: a backdrop, focus kept inside, Escape to close, and a width that no longer depends on the rail.

The month is a real table with weekday column headings, so screen readers hear the week structure; each day's button names its date.

One toolbar: view switch (Month, List), Genres and Show menus that look and save the same way, and the month controls at the end instead of in the heading area.

List view is the default on phones, instead of a grid of bare counts.
