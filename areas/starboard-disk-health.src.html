What changed and why
  • Large disk cards become one compact row per disk, sorted by tone (failing, then to check, then healthy), so nine or more disks fit on one page.
  • Each disk opens a wide SMART report slide-out: facts, problem counters, the full attribute table, self-tests and that disk's events. The full table was not available before.
  • Summary tiles (disks, to check, hottest, oldest) lead; the temperature and age charts move under the list as meters.
