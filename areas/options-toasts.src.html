Toast options

Five ways to confirm that something worked. Each is shown on a computer page and on a phone. 3 November 2026. Chosen: C, with D on settings pages.

Back to the gallery
{{i:info}}
The same in every option
  • A toast confirms a routine action in a few words: "Settings saved", "3 books added to Wanted". An error is never only a toast; it stays in the page.
  • Read out by screen readers through role="status", without moving focus.
  • Stays at least 5 seconds (today's code uses 4, below our own rule), and longer with an action: it waits while the pointer or keyboard focus is on it, and one with Undo stays until it is closed or 10 seconds pass.
  • Never covers the button that made it, the save bar or the phone's own controls.

  • Right where the person is looking; nothing to dismiss, nothing timed.
