What changed and why

Item details open in a wide slide-out, not a modal, so the queue stays in view. Previous and Next in its header replace the "Review next item after saving" checkbox.

No nesting: today the modal holds an open details block, sub-tabs, an embedded search page and an inline preview. Now Where to look is a segmented control, and results and the chosen match sit in the same slide-out.

The section toolbar replaces the hand-built bar and the "Most common problems" links: problem chips with counts, Library menu and sort.

Rows with real covers instead of a fourth card style with 9px placeholder text. "Couldn't read fully" becomes "Cannot read file". Leave unmatched and Delete stay dialogs.
