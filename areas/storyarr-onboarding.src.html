What changed and why

One wizard for setup and upgrades: the same step list, the same buttons (Continue, Back, Skip this step) and "Keep the defaults and finish" at the top. Today the two wizards use different classes, shells and button names.

One question per step ("Connect a library server?") instead of a whole Settings section with its own Save. Continue saves the answer, so nothing is lost silently.

No capitals typed in source ("STEP 1 OF 9"), no contractions ("Let's set up your library"). Done steps say "done" to screen readers, not only with a tick.

Connecting a server stays a dialog: it is a short task that must finish before the next step.
