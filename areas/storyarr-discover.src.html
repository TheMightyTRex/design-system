What changed and why

A visible h1 ("For you") replaces the hidden h1 and the slogan headings such as "Stories worth celebrating".

One shelf header for every shelf: heading, caption, View all, a More menu (Refresh, Download list, Hide from For you) and 44px arrows. Today there are four header layouts and two kinds of View all.

Customise Discover opens a slide-out, so the shelves stay in sight while you reorder them. It works while shelves load, instead of staying greyed out with no reason given.

Browse and the other views get the section toolbar: search, chips, menus, sort and an applied row with Clear all. The Lists menu sits at the end of the toolbar, not in a row of its own.
