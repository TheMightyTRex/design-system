What is new in 1.10.0

Released 28 October 2026.

Added

  • Narrator pages: open a narrator from any book to find every book they read in your library, and more by them.
  • Slide-outs: book summaries, requests and connections open beside the list, so you keep your place.

Changed

  • Settings: every section saves the same way, with a save bar that stays in view.
  • Activity: one row per request, with details in a slide-out.

Fixed

  • Notifications: deleting a channel now asks first.
Database changes and the way back

Adds a narrator table. To go back, restore the backup made in the next step from Settings, System, Upgrades and backups.
