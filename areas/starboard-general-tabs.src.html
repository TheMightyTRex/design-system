Starboard: v0.31.0

Alerts

Uptime history, alerts and a weekly summary. Inactive until you activate it, because it works with no page open.

Watching

Uptime history on each app's details needs this active.

  • Watch in the backgroundChecks your apps every few minutes with no page open, keeps their uptime history and sends the alerts you choose here. Inactive: nothing runs in the background.Costs about: 0.02 seconds of processor per check (every 5 minutes, under 0.01% overall) and under 1 MB of memory for the history. Deactivating it saves that, and keeps nothing running in the background.
  • How oftenEach check is a few small requests. Every 5 minutes is plenty for a home server.Every minute costs five times as much as every 5 minutes; every hour, a twelfth.

Where alerts go

ntfy, Gotify or a webhook. Starboard keeps the token on the server.

Choose a kind first.
Optional for ntfy and webhooks; Gotify needs an application token.

What to alert on

Each alert is sent once, and again only after it has cleared.

  • An app stops answeringAfter two checks in a row, so a restart does not alert. Another message when it answers again.
  • A disk reports a problemFrom Disk health: a failed health check, or reallocated, pending or uncorrectable sectors.
  • A folder gets fullFor the folders on System, Storage.
  • A download needs attentionWhen it has needed attention for 30 minutes.
  • The server backup failsFrom Settings, General, Backup and restore.
  • A certificate runs out soonFor apps with an outside https:// address. Checked once a day.

The server itself

Each is deactivated until you activate it. While none is active, nothing runs. It works without Watch in the background, and sends where alerts go.

  • The processor is too hotIts temperature stays at or over the limit.
  • The processor is too busyHow busy it is stays at or over the limit.
  • Memory is nearly fullThe memory in use stays at or over the limit.
  • A folder is nearly full for a long timeAny folder on System, Storage stays at or over the limit.
  • The disks are working hardAll disks together read and write at or over the limit.
  • The outside address changesChecked every 10 minutes from the No-IP updater and each VPN Starboard already asks. Starboard says it changed, never what it is, and sends nothing to any other server.Costs about: 2 milliseconds of processor a minute (0.003% overall) for all the readings together, measured with every one on, and no extra memory. Nothing runs while every one is deactivated.
  • A container stops with an errorOne list from Docker a minute. A container that was stopped on purpose, or rests, is not an alert. Another message when it runs again.Costs about: 2 milliseconds of processor a minute (0.003% overall) for all the readings together, measured with every one on, and no extra memory. Nothing runs while every one is deactivated.

Weekly summary

  • Weekly summaryOn Monday morning: how each app did, disk health and how full your folders are. Sent where alerts go.Costs about: one message a week. Nothing noticeable.
  • An update is readyOne message when a newer Starboard is out, with a link to what is new (at most once a week). One more for each plug-in or app version that waits for you. Nothing is checked for it. What updates by itself is told afterwards instead.
