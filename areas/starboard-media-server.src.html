Recommended plug-ins

Skip intros and credits, play statistics, one item for each film with several versions, and Trakt. Installed and set up through Jellyfin, one choice each.

Jellyfin 12.4.0. Starboard installs through Jellyfin's own plug-in catalogue, adding a plug-in's repository when Jellyfin does not have it. Another choice deactivates the old plug-in; nothing is uninstalled.

Skip intros and credits

A Skip button, or an automatic skip, for intros, recaps and credits. Fewer presses for keyboard, switch and remote users.

Not installed.

Skip intros and credits: what to use

Playback Reporting

Keeps a record of what each person plays, for watch statistics. The lighter watch statistics plug-in reads it, so a Full install can do without Jellystat.

Installed: Playback Reporting 19.0.0.0 Active

Playback Reporting: what to use

A few MB inside Jellyfin and a small database that grows with each play.

Merge Versions

Two copies of the same film or episode, for example HD and 4K, become one item with a choice of version.

Not installed.

Merge Versions: what to use

A few MB inside Jellyfin; a short library scan once a day in quiet hours.

Trakt

Records what each person watches on trakt.tv, and can bring their Trakt history in. Each person links their own Trakt account in Jellyfin afterwards.

Not installed.

Trakt: what to use

A few MB inside Jellyfin; a small message to Trakt when someone finishes watching.

Picture shrinker

Shrinks the posters and photos of cast and crew Jellyfin keeps, so they take less space, with a backup of each original. It runs inside Jellyfin, in its Scheduled Tasks; Starboard's own Picture shrinker plug-in stays idle while it is installed.

Not installed.

Picture shrinker: what to use

A few MB inside Jellyfin; during a run, one processor core at the lowest priority. The backup takes about as much space as the pictures did.

Pictures and details

Where Jellyfin keeps the posters, photos and details it saves.

In its settings folder, which Jellyfin watches. On a large library that uses up the computer's folder watches, and new files in Films and TV may not show until a scan.
