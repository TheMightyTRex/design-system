Covers and media cards, the A to Z list, load more, cover actions, series header and shelf.

A to Z jump list

Over a long wall of covers: # for titles that start with a number, then A to Z, from the sort title ("The Expanse" is under E, "Émile" under E). Letters with nothing are shown but cannot be chosen, and say so to screen readers. It sticks under the header; the letter in view is marked with a bar. One Tab stop: Arrow keys, Home and End move, Enter jumps and focus moves to the letter's heading. On phones the row scrolls sideways. Classes: .az-jump, .az-jump__letter, [aria-disabled="true"], .wall-letter. Behaviour: SharedComponents.azLetter(title), azJump(nav, letters), azGo(nav, letter) and the az-jump event for a wall that shows 40 at a time.

Load more as you scroll

UX-52. A long list loads its next page as its end comes into view, and keeps a real Show more button for keyboard and screen reader users. While it loads, a line says "Loading the next 40"; then a screen reader hears "40 more shown" and focus stays where it was. After three pages in a row it stops, so the footer stays in reach, and the button carries on. The button is the only way when Reduce motion is active, when the person's setting asks for it, or without script. Pressing it moves focus to the first new item. Classes: .load-more, .load-more__count, .load-more__status. Behaviour: SharedComponents.loadMore(box, { load, items, noun, pageSize, auto, maxAuto }), with reset() after a new search, filter or A to Z jump.

    When to use it

    • Long walls and lists people browse, such as a library or a history, where the order is the point.

    When not to

    • Results people compare or come back to by position, a table to sort, or a page with things after the list that people need: use pages or Show more alone.

    Keeping the footer in reach

    • It loads only when the end of the list comes into view, never on a timer, and stops after three pages in a row. The A to Z list and Back to top keep working.

    Cover actions

    UX-42. Pressing the cover or the title opens the book's Summary. A column of 44px actions runs down the cover's right edge: Details, Ebook, Audiobook, Add to a list and More. "On the cover" (the default) shows the column on hover and keyboard focus, and always on touch screens; "Beside the cover" puts it next to the art, always shown. Each person chooses in Settings, Display.

    Square show art in the book shape (FEAT-R, fill 4)

    Radio artwork is square. It sits at the top of the 2:3 shape, never cropped, with the same outline as books; the label under it takes the image's main colour (read in the browser) and carries the station and the name as real text. Without a colour the label is neutral; without an image the square shows the name.

    Behaviour

    • Tab goes to the cover ("The Salt Road, details"), then to each action in order.
    • Every icon has a full name ("Get the audiobook of The Salt Road", "Audiobook of The Salt Road in library") and a hover label that also shows on keyboard focus.
    • A format in the library is a tick on its icon plus "in library" in its name; the old badges along the bottom of the cover go.
    • Progress and errors from getting a format show in a status line under the title.
    • Titles always take two lines of space, so covers line up.

    Classes

    • .cover-card[data-actions="on"|"beside"] on a .media-card, .cover-card__open, .cover-card__art, .cover-card__status
    • .cover-actions, .cover-actions__btn, .cover-actions__tick, .cover-actions__tip
    • More: a .menu[data-fixed], so it is not cut off inside a shelf
    • Rows: .book-row (--lead), .book-row__open, .book-row__art, .book-row__text, .book-row__title, .cover-actions--row
    • Small tiles: .cover-card[data-actions="more"], .cover-card__more
    • Radio: .show-art (__img, __text, __label, __station, __name; --show-art-bg, --show-art-fg), .logo-tile (__text)

    Series header and logo

    UX-43. The top of a series (or radio show) page. A logo with a transparent background is drawn in one solid colour with a CSS mask (.logo-mask), so it follows every theme; each logo uses the text colour or the accent colour. With no logo, the name is set large as a wordmark. The name is always the page's h1. The fan of covers is decoration and is hidden on phones; the books are listed after it. The logo here is made up.

    Behaviour

    • Logos are saved once on the server and shared by everyone; admins add, change or remove them in the Series logo slide-out.
    • Three ways: From a cover (draw a box, threshold, Invert, clean up), Find online (Wikimedia Commons, with the licence and a wrong-match warning) and Upload your own.
    • In the slide-out, arrow keys move between the tabs, and on the cover arrow keys move the box (Shift and arrow keys resize it).

    Classes

    • .logo-mask (--logo, --logo-ratio, data-colour="accent"), .wordmark

    Shelf

    UX-30, option A. One row of covers that scrolls sideways, with no scroll bar. At each end the covers fade into the page, with an arrow over the fade; the arrow and fade show only when there are more books that way. On phones there are no arrows; the fade alone says there is more.

    Behaviour

    • Each arrow press moves one screen less one cover. With Reduce motion active, the shelf jumps instead of sliding.
