Help article

Help topics, guides and release notes shown in an app use .prose. The layouts and rules are in docs/HELP-WRITING.md. A topic does one task; a guide is mostly links to topics.

Help centre · Backup and restore

Back up your settings

How-to

Save a copy of Starboard's settings, so you can put them back after a new install. It takes about a minute.

Before you begin

  • Sign in as an administrator. Read Choose who can open Starboard.

Steps

  1. Go to Settings, then Backup.
  2. Select Back up now. A message says when the copy is saved.
  3. Select Download the backup (ZIP) to keep a copy on another computer.
Tip:

Activate 'Back up every night' and Starboard keeps the last 7 copies for you.

Things to note

  • A backup holds settings only.
    • Films, music and books stay on their disks and are not copied.
    • To copy those too, read Back up the server.

Move Starboard to a new computer

For anyone replacing their server. At the end, Starboard runs on the new computer with all your apps and settings. Allow about an hour.

The steps at a glance

  1. Back up your settings
  2. Install Starboard on the new computer
  3. Restore the saved copy
  4. Check your apps

Step 1: Back up your settings

Save a copy on the old computer first, while it still runs. Follow Back up your settings. You know it worked when the download finishes.

The help centre standard (owner, 14 November 2026): the pieces every help centre is built from, by tools/help_centre/build.py. Any app may use them too, such as the side tree for a long side menu or a flow chart in its own help. docs/HELP-WRITING.md section 11 says when each is used.

Side tree

A side menu that folds: areas, then groups, then every page. Built from details and summary, so it needs no script. The page you are on is marked and its area and group are open. Select an area or a group to fold it open or shut.

What is new

The headline feature of a release, in the release notes slide-out or the upgrade guide. A new feature gets How to start; an improvement does not. Describe the new state, never a comparison with before.

New feature

Know when a disk is failing

Starboard checks every disk each night and tells you before one stops working.

What is new

  • A nightly check of every disk
  • An alert when a disk needs attention
  • Each disk's history in one place

How to start

  1. Activate alerts in Settings.
  2. Go to System, then Disk health.
Open the guide: Check disk health
Improvement

Downloads now show time left

Each download says when it should finish, so you know whether to wait.

What has changed

  • Time left on every download
  • Speed in words, not arrows
  • Finished downloads move to the end

Find it in Downloads.

Nothing to set up.

Open the guide: Manage downloads

Words

  • The title is the benefit, under nine words. One sentence on what it means for the reader.
  • Three gains. The button is always "Open the guide: <topic>".

Picture
