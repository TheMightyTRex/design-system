Use

  • --color-accent for the one main action, the current page bar and links.
  • --color-text-muted for meta lines and hints, never for the only copy of something important.
  • --status-* pairs only inside chips, notices, stats and edges, always with words.

Accent

  • Teal (UX-02, option B); changing it is one token in each theme.

Scroll bars

  • Thin and square in every scrolling area, in --color-scrollbar (the border colour; the text colour in High contrast) on a clear track. They stay visible; only shelves hide theirs, behind arrows.

Chart series

  • --series-1 to --series-7 in a fixed order that follows the thing, never its rank. Checked for colour blindness, and 3:1 against the surface, in all four themes.
  • --series-empty with a hatch for free space.

Rules

  • Sentence case everywhere. No capitals for emphasis, no all-caps labels.
  • Numbers that change use .numeric (tabular figures).
  • An icon beside a word is decoration (aria-hidden); an icon alone needs a name.

Buttons

44px high, square, bold 0.875rem. One primary button per view. Words say what happens: "Save connection", not "OK".

Variants

  • .btn--primary: the one main action.
  • .btn--quiet: Cancel and secondary links.
  • .btn--danger: removing; asks first in a dialog.
  • .btn--compact: 32px, inside dense tables only.

Accessibility

  • Icon-only buttons have a name that includes the thing: "More actions for Dune".
  • Disabled buttons say why nearby, or stay enabled and explain on press.

Menu button

Related actions in one button instead of a row of them (the Lists menu). Opens with focus on the first item; arrow keys move; Escape closes.

Use

  • For actions, use menuitem; for one choice from a list (sort), menuitemradio. For a form value, use a select instead.
  • Keep the button's name the same while it works ("Lists"); say progress in a status line.

Classes

  • .menu-wrap, .menu, .menu__label, [data-align="start"]

Shapes

  • Success, info and neutral: a dot. Warning: a diamond. Danger: a square. Muted: a ring.
  • High contrast adds a border to every chip.

Chip style

  • Outline with an icon (style B, chosen on the UX-04 board).

Where status goes

  • A heading: .title-status, chips straight after the name, centred on its line. Never on a line of their own under it. Details such as a version go on the right.
  • A list of things with a state: .status-icon straight after the name, the word in hidden text and a tooltip.
  • A row with actions: the chip in .row__side. A card: in .card__meta. A stat tile: under the value. A cover: its corner.
  • At most two chips in one place; more belongs in a facts list.

Slide-out

From the right, over a dimmed page. For the details of one thing, and for editing it, without losing your place in the list. Its header wears the page background (--color-slide-header) and its body is the raised surface: white in Light, the dark surface in Dark. The phone menu, which slides from the left, looks the same.

Behaviour

  • Focus moves to Close; Tab stays inside; Escape, Close or a press outside closes it; focus returns to what opened it.
  • The page behind does not scroll. Its address can include the open item, so Back closes it.
  • Editing: Save and Cancel in a footer that stays put; Remove on the far right, which asks first.

Sizes

  • 420px; .side-panel--wide 640px for logs or long forms; full width on phones.

Colour

  • Header: the page background, so it reads as part of the page. Body and footer: the raised surface. Dialogs keep the plain header, so a slide-out and a dialog look different at a glance.
  • High contrast keeps a solid line under the header.

Classes

  • .side-panel-layer, .side-panel-backdrop, .side-panel, .panel-header, .side-panel__body, .side-panel__foot, .side-panel__lead

Dialog

A modal, only for a decision or a short task that must finish before going on: confirming a removal, adding a list, a password check. Its header is plain (the panel header colour), unlike a slide-out's.
